# Authority And Review

## Ceiling

The skill may qualify only whether registered evidence can support a bounded observational comparison. It does not validate environmental assets or establish causality, carbon quantity, additionality, ACCU/project validity, quality, integrity, permanence, compliance, legal liability, greenwashing, or financial suitability.

A request outside that ceiling follows the controlled refusal path: `REFUSED`, null evidence disposition, `AUTHORITY_SCOPE_EXCEEDED`, and human review required.

## Mandatory Review

Stop for human or specialist review before:

- freezing or changing temporal windows, seasonal matching, SCL rules, coverage thresholds, or other scientific policy;
- substituting an unregistered evidence source;
- changing the authority ceiling or forbidden-inference registry;
- treating partial evidence as a completed assessment;
- making legal, regulatory, assurance, audit, credit-quality, lending, trading, investment, or tokenisation conclusions;
- enabling network retrieval or any external side effect.

Do not infer a favorable environmental or financial conclusion from contract validity.
