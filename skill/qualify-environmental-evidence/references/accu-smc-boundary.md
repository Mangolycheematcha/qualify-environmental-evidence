# ACCU And SMC Boundary

The Clean Energy Regulator describes an ACCU as representing one tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent emissions that would otherwise have been released into the atmosphere. It describes an SMC as a unit associated with a Safeguard facility's emissions below its baseline and explicitly distinguishes SMCs as accounting units that are not offsets.

The shared one-tonne unit does not establish semantic, environmental, legal, quality, price, or market equivalence. Do not infer that ACCUs and SMCs are interchangeable, equally suitable, equally risky, substitutable in a transaction, or supported by the same underlying evidence.

Allowed output is limited to cited public definitions and clearly stated differences. Refuse requests for unit-quality ranking, price or liquidity prediction, automated substitution, surrender strategy, trading, tokenisation, regulatory compliance, or financial action. Route those questions to appropriately authorised legal, regulatory, market, methodology, or financial specialists.
