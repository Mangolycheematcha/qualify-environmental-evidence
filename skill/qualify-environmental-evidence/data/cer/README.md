# CER Public Evidence Snapshots

These files are small, curated, source-attributed fact snapshots used by the offline qualification demo. They are not raw CER pages, complete register exports, legal records, or substitutes for checking the current official source.

- `eop101132-project-record.json` records selected public facts from the CER project page as accessed on 2026-09-05.
- `accu-smc-public-definitions.json` records the CER's public distinction between ACCUs and SMCs as accessed on 2026-09-05.

The snapshots preserve canonical source URLs and retrieval dates. They contain no credentials, personal contact details, signed URLs, raster data, or complete source payloads. Their inclusion does not imply CER endorsement. CER material remains subject to its applicable terms; the repository's Apache-2.0 licence does not relicense it.
