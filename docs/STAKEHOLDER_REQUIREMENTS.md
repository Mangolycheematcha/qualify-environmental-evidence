# Stakeholder requirements matrix

Reviewed 2026-09-05. “Requirement” below means an official legal, regulatory, scheme, or institutional statement. “Engineering response” is this repository's interpretation and is not legal advice, regulator approval, or proof of production readiness.

| Stakeholder | Official source and source class | Source-grounded need | Engineering response | Current evidence | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CER and public-data users | [CER ACCU project and contract register](https://cer.gov.au/markets/reports-and-data/accu-project-and-contract-register), official scheme publication | Public project descriptions, locations, type, issuance, relinquishment and obligations must retain their published meaning | Exact canonical-URI allowlist, entity-scoped facts, source access date/hash, no project-quality inference | Seven real project records across six method types | Periodic source refresh and API-level regression test |
| Safeguard data users | [CER 2024-25 baselines and emissions data](https://cer.gov.au/markets/reports-and-data/safeguard-data/2024-25-baselines-and-emissions-data), official scheme publication | Facility-period baselines, covered/net emissions, ACCU/SMC adjustments and SMC issuance are distinct published fields | Separate `SAFEGUARD_FACILITY` entity and claim family; Arcadia row retained as a facility-period fact | One real 2024-25 record and exact raw CSV hash | Broader facility sample and update monitoring |
| Environmental-claim reviewers | [ACCC environmental claims guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/about-us/publications/a-guide-to-making-environmental-claims-for-business), regulator guidance | Claims should be accurate, evidence-backed, clear, qualified and not omit material information | Bounded statements, citations, limitations, abstention and prohibited broad claims | Deterministic claim ceiling and adversarial refusals | Legal review for any customer-facing use |
| Financial product issuers | [ASIC INFO 271](https://asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-services/how-to-avoid-greenwashing-when-offering-or-promoting-sustainability-related-products/), regulator information sheet | Headline sustainability representations must not be misleading; qualifications cannot cure a misleading headline | No financial-product recommendation, credit-quality rating or greenwashing conclusion; human review always required | Refusal families and security evaluation | Regulated-use policy, disclosure review and accountable owner |
| APRA-regulated institutions | [APRA CPS 230](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-230), in-force prudential standard | Operational risks, controls, service providers, incidents, continuity and information-system capability require management and testing | Deterministic replay, control tests, incident history and explicit provider boundary | Local security/performance tests and frozen provenance | Authentication, service-provider due diligence, BCP, monitoring, incident operations and CPS 234 assessment |
| Microsoft engineering reviewers | [Microsoft Agent Framework overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/agent-framework/overview/) and [2026 Responsible AI transparency report](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/topics/responsible-ai/reports/transparency-report/), official product and governance material | Choose the minimum sufficient abstraction; assess risk, safeguards, human oversight and performance | Framework ADR, deterministic pre-gates, explicit human-review flag, evaluation denominators | ADR 0001, 80-case engineering benchmark, security and latency reports | Live provider eval, deployment telemetry and phased operational testing |
| DFCRC and tokenised-finance researchers | [DFCRC research mission](https://dfcrc.com.au/) and [RBA/DFCRC Project Acacia](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/central-bank-digital-currency/wholesale-central-bank-digital-currency.html), institutional research | Tokenised assets and settlement research examines feasibility, risks, operating models and infrastructure; it does not establish this asset's eligibility | `TRADING_OR_TOKENISATION` is refused; outputs may be research inputs only | ACCU/SMC boundary and no-transfer tests | Legal, market-infrastructure, asset-rights and settlement research outside this PoC |
| Academic and independent reviewers | [ACM artifact review and badging](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current), scholarly evaluation policy | Artifacts should be documented, consistent, complete, exercisable and, where claimed, reproducible | Canonical JSON, hashes, frozen split, runnable skill/demo, generated artifact checks | Offline replay and checked evaluation outputs | Independent replication and human-labelled H1 |

## Non-transfer rule

Registry publication is not project integrity assurance. A bounded EO observation is not carbon quantification. An SMC facility-period record is not an ACCU project record. A research interest in tokenisation is not evidence of legal, technical, commercial, or investment readiness.
